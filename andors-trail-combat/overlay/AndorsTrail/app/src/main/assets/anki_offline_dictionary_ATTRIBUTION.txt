Offline Anki Vietnamese-English Dictionary

VNEDICT by Paul Denisowski.
Source: http://www.denisowski.org/Vietnamese/vnedict_readme.htm
Repackaged StarDict data from:
https://github.com/cer28/stardict-vnedict
Pinned upstream snapshot:
https://github.com/cer28/stardict-vnedict/tree/96f54e16f1ed1062a4b6c817711f3b097e477e2d

Adaptations: extracted StarDict headwords and English glosses into UTF-8
lookup tables, stripped formatting, shortened long entries to fit an
in-game popup, and supplemented common Vietnamese function words.
The dictionary is bundled in the APK and works entirely offline.
It is not a full-sentence machine translation model.

The Vietnamese compound segmentation word list is by
Vietnamese Natural Language Processing (VNTK), MIT license.
https://github.com/vntk/dictionary
See anki_vietnamese_compounds_LICENSE.txt.
